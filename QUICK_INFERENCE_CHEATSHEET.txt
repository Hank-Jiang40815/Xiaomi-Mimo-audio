⚙️ 配置細節
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  模型:         MiMo-Audio-7B-Base
  Tokenizer:    MiMo-Audio-Tokenizer
  方法:         40-shot In-Context Learning
  訓練範例:     optical_denoised (001-040)
  裝置:         CUDA (GPU)
  Max tokens:   8192

  Instruction:
  "Enhance the audio quality and remove noise from the input speech.
   IMPORTANT: You MUST preserve the exact original speech content and
   transcript. The speech content is exactly 10 Chinese characters.
   Only improve the audio quality, do not change any words."


🔧 故障排除
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

問題                          解決方案
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
找不到輸入檔案                確認路徑正確，使用絕對路徑
找不到模型                    檢查 models/ 目錄是否有模型檔案
找不到訓練範例                確認 examples/optical_denoised/ 存在
CUDA out of memory            檢查 nvidia-smi，關閉其他程式
權限錯誤                      chmod +x quick_inference_expA+.sh


💡 使用技巧
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. 📁 輸出整理
   建立有意義的目錄結構:
   $ mkdir -p results/exp_A+/$(date +%Y%m%d)
   $ ./quick_inference_expA+.sh input.wav results/exp_A+/$(date +%Y%m%d)/out.wav

2. 📝 記錄轉錄
   將 stdout 導向檔案保存轉錄結果:
   $ ./quick_inference_expA+.sh input.wav | tee inference.log

3. 🚀 背景執行
   處理大量檔案時:
   $ nohup ./quick_inference_expA+.sh input.wav &

4. ⏱️  計時
   測量推論時間:
   $ time ./quick_inference_expA+.sh input.wav
